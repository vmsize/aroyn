Project and contact

Aroyn is a free independent project maintained by vmsize from Ukraine. Contact: septave on Discord. Find the username through Discord’s Add Friend screen and send a private message. Do not include passwords, dashboard keys, access tokens or identity documents.

This notice describes the Aroyn test version. It does not replace Discord’s or Roblox’s privacy policies.

Data and purposes

DataPurpose
Discord ID, username, display name and avatar referenceCreate and identify your Aroyn account and display its profile. Sign-in requests only identify; it does not request your email, server list or messages.
Aroyn session and dashboard-key recordsAuthorize access, expire sessions and revoke credentials. Server records contain credential hashes, timestamps and a short dashboard-key suffix. Your browser holds the session token for authorized requests.
Linked Roblox ID, names, avatar and first/last seen timesShow linked runtimes and select the account whose telemetry you want to view. A client-supplied ID is not proof that someone owns a Roblox account.
Runtime session, activity, inventory and statisticsDisplay information sent by the compatible runtime. The exact fields depend on what the client sends.
Session history: session/Roblox IDs, version, game/place, supplied device type, timestamps and connection statusShow activity and help understand service usage and connection problems. Identifiable history is different from aggregate counts.
Theme, accent, sidebar, log filters and configuration preferencesRemember choices in this browser. See Cookies & storage.

Aroyn’s sign-in does not ask for your Discord password. Hosting and external services can receive your IP address and connection metadata even though Aroyn’s reviewed database has no dedicated IP-address field.

How long data stays

Runtime snapshots7 days after their last stored update.
Aroyn profile and linked accountsUntil you delete the account or remove a link. Link-revocation markers remain until relinking or account deletion.
Temporary presence15 minutes of inactivity.
Public Roblox profile cache30 days after its last update.
Web sessionUp to 30 days, or earlier logout/revocation.
Aggregate counts without account IDsMay be retained.

Scheduled cleanup runs daily; expired data is removed on the next successful run, not at an exact deadline. Hosting logs, backups and other providers’ caches have separate settings that are still being reviewed for the test deployment.

Export, delete or ask for corrections

Open your profile menu → Account data. Download exports profile fields, linked accounts, explicitly associated history and saved account snapshots. Access credentials are excluded. Deletion requires a Discord sign-in within the last 15 minutes and typing DELETE.

  • Deleting your Aroyn account revokes access and removes its owned data. It does not delete your Discord or Roblox account.
  • If cleanup is pending, access remains revoked and the deletion retries. A pending result does not mean deletion is complete.
  • Signing in after completed deletion creates a new empty Aroyn account.
  • Older unassigned history cannot be exported just by matching a Roblox ID. It expires on the history schedule; legacy v1 snapshots are excluded from export.
  • Downloaded files and local copies on other devices cannot be remotely erased. Application deletion does not promise immediate deletion of provider logs or backups.

Contact septave on Discord for corrections, a processing objection, a parent/guardian request or a data problem the page cannot resolve. Identify the affected account without sending secrets. We need to verify that the requester is entitled to act before disclosing or changing account data. Your rights under applicable law are unaffected.

External services

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and backend storage.
  • Discord handles sign-in; profile avatars can load from Discord’s CDN.
  • Roblox provides profile and avatar information; avatars may load in the dashboard.
  • Backend owner analytics can query ScriptBlox for aggregate script information. This is a server request, not a homepage embed.

These services can receive connection metadata and process it under their own terms and settings. Processing may occur outside your country. Provider arrangements, logging and international-transfer details need confirmation before an open release.

Teen users

Aroyn’s intended audience includes teens who are eligible to use Discord in their country. If you need a parent or guardian’s permission, ask them to read these notices with you. Aroyn does not ask for your date of birth or identity documents in this version. Discord sign-in does not itself prove that a user meets every age or guardian requirement.
