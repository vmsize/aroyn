About Aroyn

Aroyn is a free independent project maintained by vmsize from Ukraine. Contact: septave on Discord. Find the username through Discord’s Add Friend screen. If a private message is unavailable, send a friend request first; after it is accepted, send your message privately. Do not include passwords, dashboard keys, access tokens or identity documents.

Aroyn displays telemetry from a compatible Roblox runtime. It is an independent project and does not claim endorsement by Discord, Roblox or game developers. The homepage screenshot is an illustrative example, not a live account or a promise of results.

Who can use it

The audience includes eligible teen users. You must meet Discord’s age rules, including a minimum age of 13 and any higher requirement in your country. Where you cannot agree independently, a parent or legal guardian must review and agree on your behalf. Do not bypass age restrictions. This version does not collect birth dates or identity documents to verify age.

Account and permitted use

  • Use only accounts and runtimes you are entitled to operate. Follow applicable laws and the rules of Discord, Roblox and the experience you use.
  • Keep your dashboard key and session token private. Regenerate a disclosed key, sign out of affected sessions and contact the maintainer if access is compromised.
  • Do not access others’ accounts, expose others’ data, overload the service or use it to distribute malicious content.

Free service and availability

Aroyn currently has no paid features, subscriptions or payment forms, so it offers no paid-service refund process. If payments are introduced, payment and refund terms must be stated before a purchase.

This is a test version. Features can be unavailable or change during testing. No uptime, support response time, gameplay outcome or protection from third-party account restrictions is promised. These terms do not remove rights that applicable law does not allow to be waived.

Privacy and leaving

Read the Privacy notice and Cookies & storage before sign-in. Signing in is not consent to advertising or unrelated processing.

You can stop using Aroyn, sign out or use Account data to export/delete your Aroyn account. Pending cleanup is shown separately from completed deletion. Discord/Roblox accounts and downloaded files are not deleted.

Code and brand

Original project code is licensed under MIT with attribution to vmsize. That code licence does not grant permission to use Aroyn names, the logo or third-party materials. Do not represent an unofficial fork as the official service.

Questions and updates

For support, account issues or vulnerability reports, privately contact septave on Discord. Do not publish credentials or sensitive reports. This preview is dated 2 October 2026; material changes should be explained before they apply.
